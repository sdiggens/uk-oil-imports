How did the UK's oil and diesel imports shift after Russia was cut off in 2022, and why do the official sources disagree about where they come from?

## Findings so far
1. Since the bans, the US has increased oil exports to the UK by 56% by weight. About a fifth is refined fuel, which is why it ranks first by value but second by weight.
2. Despite losing Russia, the UK's crude supply became more diverse, rising from about 4.1 effective supplier countries in 2021 to 5.0 in 2025. 2022 was the most volatile year.
